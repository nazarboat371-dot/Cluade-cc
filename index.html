Chapter 04 — The Vessel

Why the jar matters

Plastic pouches leach. Cans taste of tin. Glass keeps a sauce exactly as we made it — bright, clean, and a little bit rebellious.

  1. 01

    Infinitely recyclable

    Glass can be melted and reborn forever without losing a thing. Rinse it, bin it, or keep it.

  2. 02

    Zero plastic taste

    Acidic tomato and plastic don't mix. Glass is inert, so you only taste tomato, chili and cream.

  3. 03

    Sealed at peak simmer

    We hot-fill every jar straight from the pot and seal it in seconds. No preservatives needed.

  4. 04

    Made for a second life

    Our ribbed lid fits a spice drawer perfectly. Customers send us their jar shelfies weekly.
